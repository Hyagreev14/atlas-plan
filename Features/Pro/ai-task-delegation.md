# AI Task Delegation

**Release:** ATLAS Pro  
**Status:** Planned

## Purpose
Break larger development tasks into subtasks and coordinate supported providers or agents.

## Boundaries
Manage shared context, dependencies and conflicting edits; delegation must not multiply or bypass permissions.
